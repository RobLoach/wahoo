🐰 Wahoo

A 4-player team race. Stomp your rivals, tuck your bunnies safely into the burrow.

Local Game

Hot seat and/or CPU players on this device.

Online Game

Hosted right in your browser — share the code with friends. A free relay sets up the connection; on the same network the game then runs peer-to-peer.

Defaults to https://wahoo.robloach.net (PHP relay). You can also self-host: the PHP server from server/wahoo-php on any web host, or the Node WebSocket server via npm run server (use a ws:// address for that one).

House Rules

Applies to games you start or host — guests play by the host's rules.

How to play
  • Teams of two sit opposite each other (Red & Green vs Blue & Yellow). First team to get all 8 bunnies home wins.
  • Each round you're dealt 4 cards. On your turn, play one card and do what it says — no drawing back up. If nothing in your hand is playable, you fold for the round.
  • A / 2: spawn a bunny (2 also flips a bonus card). 4: move backward 4 spaces (staying on the track). 7: move one bunny 7, or split the 7 between two bunnies. J: swap with any bunny. Q: 12 forward. K: move 13 forward, or spawn from your reserve onto any other player's bunny — even a teammate's — stomping it. Other cards move their face value.
  • Landing on any bunny stomps it back to its reserve. Burrow entry needs an exact count, and there's no jumping inside the burrow — the slots you pass must be open. Bunnies in burrows are safe from everything.

How to play

  • Teams of two sit opposite each other (✦ Red & Green vs ● Blue & Yellow). First team to get all 8 bunnies into their burrows wins.
  • Each round every player is dealt 4 cards. On your turn, play one card and do what it says — no drawing back up. If nothing in your hand is playable, you fold for the round.
  • Landing on any bunny stomps it back to its owner's reserve; passing through is safe. Burrow entry needs an exact count, with no jumping inside the burrow — the slots you pass must be open. Bunnies in burrows are safe from everything.
  • Once your own 4 bunnies are home you keep receiving cards and move your teammate's bunnies on your turn.

Card reference

ASpawn a bunny onto your corner space, or move one bunny forward 1.
2Spawn a bunny or move 2 — then flip the top card of the draw pile and play it too.
3 5 6 8 9 10Move one bunny forward that many spaces.
4Move one bunny backward 4 spaces (stays on the track).
7Move one bunny 7, or split the 7 between two bunnies.
JSwap one of your bunnies with any other bunny on the track.
QMove one bunny forward 12 spaces.
KMove one bunny forward 13, or spawn from your reserve onto another player's bunny, stomping it.
