Wahoo

Four bunnies each. Stomp your rivals, tuck your own safely home.

Local Game

Hot seat and CPU players on this device.

Online Game

Host in your browser, or use a server.

Hosted right in your browser — share the five-letter code. A free relay sets up the connection, then the game runs peer-to-peer. Empty seats are filled by CPUs.

Defaults to https://wahoo.robloach.net (PHP relay). You can also self-host: the PHP server from server/wahoo-php on any web host, or the Node WebSocket server via npm run server (use a ws:// address for that one).

House Rules

Applies to games you start or host — guests play by the host's rules.

How to play

The rules in brief. The full card reference is one tap away in every game.

  • Teams of two sit opposite each other — Red & Green against Blue & Yellow. First team to get all eight bunnies home wins.
  • Every round you're dealt four cards. Play one on your turn and do what it says — no drawing back up. If nothing is playable, you fold for the round.
  • A / 2: spawn a bunny (2 also flips a bonus card). 4: move backward 4 spaces, staying on the track. 7: move one bunny 7, or split the 7 between two bunnies. J: swap with any bunny. Q: 12 forward. K: move 13 forward, or spawn from your reserve onto any other player's bunny — even a teammate's — stomping it. Other cards move their face value.
  • Landing on any bunny stomps it back to its reserve; passing through is safe. Burrow entry needs an exact count, and there's no jumping inside — every slot you pass must be open. Bunnies in the burrow are safe from everything.

All eight bunnies home

How to play

  • Teams of two sit opposite each other — Red & Green against Blue & Yellow. First team to get all eight bunnies home wins.
  • Every round you're dealt four cards. Play one on your turn and do what it says — no drawing back up. If nothing is playable, you fold for the round.
  • Landing on any bunny stomps it back to its reserve; passing through is safe.
  • Burrow entry needs an exact count, and there's no jumping inside — every slot you pass must be open. Bunnies in the burrow are safe from everything.
  • Once your own four bunnies are home you keep receiving cards and move your teammate's bunnies on your turn.
