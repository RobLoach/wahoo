🐰 Wahoo

A 4-player team race. Stomp your rivals, tuck your bunnies safely into the burrow.

Local Game

Hot seat and/or CPU players on this device.

Online Game

Online play needs a Wahoo server (npm run server in the repo). GitHub Pages hosts only this client.

How to play
  • Teams of two sit opposite each other (Red & Green vs Blue & Yellow). First team to get all 8 bunnies home wins.
  • Each round you're dealt 4 cards. On your turn, play one card and do what it says — no drawing back up. If nothing in your hand is playable, you fold for the round.
  • A / 2: spawn a bunny (2 also flips a bonus card). 4: move backward — you can back straight into your burrow. 7: split 7 steps across your bunnies. J: swap with any bunny. Q: 12 forward. K: move 13 forward, or leap one of your bunnies onto any other player's bunny — even a teammate's — squashing it. Other cards move their face value.
  • Landing on any bunny stomps it back to its reserve. Burrow entry needs an exact count, and there's no jumping inside the burrow — the slots you pass must be open. Bunnies in burrows are safe from everything.
